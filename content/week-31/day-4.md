## Revelation

Before Jesus gave the commission, He established the authority. All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me — therefore go. The therefore is the hinge. The going is not a suggestion from someone hoping you will cooperate. It is the command of the One who holds all authority in every realm that exists.

This changes everything about how you go.

You are not going as a volunteer who signed up for a cause that may or may not succeed. You are not going in your own strength, with your own resources, hoping the message lands. You are going as a sent person — commissioned by the One who has already won, backed by authority that has no ceiling, accompanied by a presence that will not leave you at the border of the hard place.

The commission is not only for missionaries and pastors. It is for everyone who has encountered Jesus and been changed by the encounter. You were not saved to sit. You were saved to go — into your family, your workplace, your neighbourhood, your city. To make disciples. To bear fruit that lasts.

And the blessing attached to the commission is the most important part: I am with you always, to the very end of the age. Not I will check in on you. Not I will be available if you need Me. Always. To the very end. The same presence that raised Jesus from the dead goes with you into every conversation, every nation, every hard place the commission takes you.

You have been chosen and appointed to go and bear fruit. Go with confidence. The authority behind you is total. The presence with you is permanent.

## Morning Prayer

Father, I receive today both the commission and the blessing — the call to go and the promise that You go with me. I have been sitting with the encounter too long without letting it send me. Today I go — not in my own strength, not with my own authority, but as a sent person carrying the name and the presence of the One who holds all authority. Show me who in my immediate world needs what I carry. And let me go with the confidence of someone who knows exactly whose authority is behind them.

## Daily Practices

- Read Matthew 28:16–20 in full — the complete Great Commission. Note that some of the disciples doubted even as they worshipped (verse 17). Jesus commissioned them anyway. Ask: what doubt am I using as a reason not to go — and does Jesus's commissioning wait for the doubt to be resolved?
- Journal: "Who in my immediate world — family, colleague, neighbour — has God specifically placed in my path to bear fruit toward? What is one concrete step of the commission I can take toward that person this week?" Write the specific name and the specific step.
- Read Acts 1:8 — you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses. Note the sequence: receive first, then go. Ask: am I trying to go on my own resources, or am I going from the fullness of what I have received?
