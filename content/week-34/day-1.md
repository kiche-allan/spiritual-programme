## Revelation

What do you do when you can't sense God moving anymore?

When Jesus was baptised, John saw the Spirit of God descend on Him like a dove. Now, I don't know how familiar you are with doves, but it doesn't take much to make them fly away. A small movement here or there, and they're gone.

The Holy Spirit is the sensitive One of the Trinity. If you belong to Christ, He lives in you, and He will never leave you. You received Him fully the moment you were saved. But you can grieve Him, so that you lose the sense of His presence, His power and His leading. Paul warns us plainly: "Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God." And right after that verse he lists what grieves Him: bitterness, rage, anger, slander and malice.

Here's what many of us do when that happens. We try to work Him up. Some people think that if we really want the Spirit to move, we must lift our hands higher. Others think, "We'd better start jumping." Now, I'm all for lifted hands and dancing feet. There's a time for both. But He doesn't come down because we worship harder. If we have grieved Him, jumping higher won't bring the dove back.

Look at David. After his sin with Bathsheba, he didn't try to sing his way back into God's presence. He prayed, "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me." In the very next verse he cried, "Cast me not away from your presence, and take not your Holy Spirit from me." He knew what he had lost, and he knew what to ask for. A clean heart is the way back.

We don't need more of the Spirit. We need to stop offending the Spirit we already have.

Lifting your hands in worship is a wonderful thing. But it was never meant to replace repentance. A loud Sunday can't cover a quiet sin. You can raise your hands in church and still be hiding a secret, nursing a grudge or telling a lie, and the dove has noticed.

So before you walk into worship this Sunday, get quiet and ask the Lord, "Is there anything in me that has grieved You?" Whatever He brings to mind, name it, confess it right then, and turn from it. Then go and lift your hands with a clean heart.

## Morning Prayer

Holy Spirit, forgive me for the times I have grieved You and then tried to make up for it with noise. I don't want more excitement. I want a clean heart. Show me what has offended You, and give me the courage to confess it and turn away from it. Renew a right spirit in me, and let me sense Your presence again.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read Psalm 51 slowly and Ephesians 4:29–32. List the things Paul says grieve the Spirit, and mark the ones you recognise in yourself.
- Journal: "Is there anything in me that has grieved the Holy Spirit?" Write down whatever He brings to mind, then write beside it: "Confessed and turned from."
- Before this Sunday's worship, spend five quiet minutes with no music, asking God to search your heart. Confess before you sing.
