## Revelation

When did you stop being amazed by Him?

The church at Ephesus had an impressive record. Hard workers. Doctrinally sound. They tested false teachers and did not tolerate wickedness. By every external measure they were doing everything right. And Jesus still had one thing against them: they had left their first love.

Somewhere along the way the fire had cooled. The wonder had faded. The work continued but the worship had gone hollow. They were still performing the religion without feeling the relationship.

That can happen to any of us — not in one dramatic moment but gradually, quietly, through the accumulation of busyness and distraction and familiarity. We keep serving without marvelling. We keep attending without encountering. We keep going through the motions long after the motion has lost its meaning.

Stay in love with Jesus. Not with a denomination. Not with a ministry. Not even with a church. With Him. If you stay in love with Jesus everything else follows — the service, the worship, the generosity, the mission. They are all downstream of the first love. When the first love is gone, everything else becomes obligation.

And if the fire has dimmed — if you are reading this and you recognise the drift — there is a simple answer. Repent and return. Go back to where it started. Remember what it felt like when you first understood what He did for you and let that remembrance be the beginning of the rekindling.

He is waiting. The love that started this is still there on His end. Fall in love again.

## Morning Prayer

Father, I confess that I have been doing the right things without the right heart behind them — serving without wonder, attending without encounter, going through the motions without the love that makes the motions mean something. Forgive me for leaving my first love. Today I return to where it started — to the simple, overwhelming truth that You loved me and gave Yourself for me. Rekindle what has cooled. I want to be amazed by You again.

## Daily Practices

- Read Revelation 2:1–7 in full — Jesus's full address to the church at Ephesus. Note verse 5: remember the height from which you have fallen, repent, and do the things you did at first. Ask: what did your relationship with Jesus look like at its most alive — and what specific thing would returning to that look like today?
- Journal: "Where has my walk with Jesus become more routine than relationship — more performance than passion? What is the one thing I did at first that I have stopped doing?" Write the honest answer and take one step back toward it today.
- Read Song of Songs 1:4 — take me away with you, let us hurry. The language of someone who cannot get enough of the presence of the Beloved. Ask: does that describe my current posture toward Jesus — and if not, what has replaced it?
