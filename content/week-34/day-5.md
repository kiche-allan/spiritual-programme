## Revelation

If Jesus came back today, would it catch you off guard?

A thief doesn't send a notice. He doesn't call ahead to say he will be passing by on Thursday. Think about why we put grilles on our windows, lights over our gates and askaris at our estates. It's because nobody who has ever been broken into has said, "I saw that coming." That's the whole point.

Paul says that for the world, the day of the Lord is going to come just like that. Sudden. Unexpected. But then he turns to believers and says, "Not you."

Now, that doesn't mean we get to know the date. Over the years, plenty of people have tried to set one, and every one of them has been wrong. Jesus said that no one knows the day or the hour. So why won't it surprise us? Because we don't live in the dark. When you are a child of the day, you are dressed and ready whenever the knock comes.

Then Paul gives two instructions: stay awake and be sober.

Staying awake means not drifting into spiritual sleep. You can sit in church every week and still be sound asleep on the inside, going through the motions and numb to the things of God. Being sober means keeping a clear head. It means not getting so intoxicated by this world, with its money, its entertainment and its worries, that you lose sight of what's coming. Paul says it again in Romans: "the hour has come for you to wake from sleep. For salvation is nearer to us now than when we first believed."

Here's the question I want you to ask yourself. Is there anything in my life right now that I would be ashamed for Jesus to walk in on? If there is, that is a part of your life that is still living in the dark.

So switch on the lights. Bring it to Him and confess it. Wake up to what He is doing around you. You are a child of the day. Live like it, and His coming won't be a threat. It will be the best day of your life.

## Morning Prayer

Father, thank You that I don't belong to the darkness. Thank You that I'm a child of the day. Wake me from every kind of sleep: the numbness, the distraction and the secret sin. Keep my mind clear, and let me live today in a way that I wouldn't be ashamed for Jesus to see.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11 and Romans 13:11–14. Notice how Paul links being awake with how we live.
- Journal: "Is there anything in my life that I'd be ashamed for Jesus to walk in on?" Write it down, confess it to God, and write what you will do about it this week.
- Pick one thing that has been numbing you spiritually (a habit, a show, a screen, a worry) and set it aside for the whole day.
