## Revelation

How do you see God?

Psychologists tell us that our first image of God is filtered through our relationship with our earthly father. And for many of us that is a serious problem.

If your father was absent you probably assume God is distant. If he was harsh you expect God to be waiting to punish you. If he made promises he never kept you struggle to trust that God will follow through. Unconsciously, you have projected every wound your father left onto the character of God — and you have been relating to a version of Him that does not exist.

But God is not your earthly father.

Your heavenly Father does not leave. He does not abandon. He does not change His mind about you when you fail. Romans 8:38–39 is the most comprehensive statement of security in the New Testament — nothing in all creation can separate you from His love. Not your worst season. Not your worst decision. Not the version of yourself you are most ashamed of.

Your earthly father may have failed you. That wound is real. But the healing begins when you stop seeing God through the lens of that wound and start seeing Him through the lens of His Word.

He loves you. He is not leaving. You are secure.

## Morning Prayer

Father, I confess that I have been seeing You through the lens of every person who let me down rather than through the lens of Your Word. Today I choose to receive what You actually say about Yourself — near, faithful, and unable to stop loving me. Heal the distorted picture. Let me see You as You are. And let me see myself the way You see me — as Your child, secure, held, and loved.

## Daily Practices

- Read Psalm 27:10 — though my father and mother forsake me, the Lord will receive me. Bring the specific wound of an earthly relationship before God and ask Him to speak His own character into that place.
- Journal: "Where have I been projecting onto God the failures of the people who were supposed to represent Him? What does His Word say about Himself in that specific area?" Write the honest comparison.
- Read Romans 8:31–39 slowly. Note every specific thing Paul says cannot separate you from God's love. Receive each one as addressed personally to you today.
