## Revelation

How many times have you prayed for something and then forgotten to say thank you when it came?

Ten men with leprosy stood at a distance and cried out, "Jesus, Master, have pity on us!" They were outcasts, shut out of villages and family homes, and they had nothing to offer Him except their need. Jesus didn't touch them or pray a long prayer. He simply said, "Go, show yourselves to the priests." And as they went, they were cleansed. All ten.

Now watch what happens next. Nine kept walking. They had what they came for. Maybe they were rushing to the priest to be declared clean so they could go home, hug their children and get their lives back. I understand that. You'd run too. But one man stopped, turned around and came back, praising God in a loud voice. He threw himself at Jesus' feet and thanked Him. Luke adds one more detail: he was a Samaritan. The outsider, the one the others would have looked down on, was the only one who remembered.

Jesus asked three questions: "Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine? Has no one returned to give praise to God except this foreigner?" Don't miss the sadness in them. The healing didn't fail. The gratitude did.

And notice that Jesus said something more to the one who returned: "Rise and go; your faith has made you well." The nine were cleansed in their skin. The one who came back was made whole. Thanksgiving takes a blessing and turns it into a relationship.

Let's be honest. Many of us are the nine. We pray hard when the school fees are due, when the results are pending, when the job interview is next week. Then the answer comes, and we're busy. We tell the story to our friends and forget to tell it to Him.

So today, think of one thing you asked God for that He has already answered. It may be small. Go back to Him, not with a request, but with a loud, wholehearted "asante." Be the one who turned around.

## Morning Prayer

Father, forgive me for the many times I have asked, received and walked on. Thank You for the prayers You have already answered, the big ones and the quiet ones I forgot to mention. Today I turn around and come back to Your feet. Asante, Lord. Make me a person who remembers.

## Daily Practices

- Read Luke 17:11–19 slowly. Ask: which of the ten am I most like, the one who kept walking or the one who came back?
- Journal: "What did I ask God for in the last year that He has answered? Have I thanked Him properly?" Write down three answered prayers.
- Go back to God today with one answered prayer and give thanks out loud, with no request attached.
