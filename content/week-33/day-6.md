## Revelation

When did you last sit down and count what God has done for you this year?

We're moving towards the end of the year, and most of us are already thinking about what is next: plans, targets, resolutions. David does something wiser first. He talks to himself: "Praise the Lord, my soul, and forget not all his benefits." Look at the instruction. Not "learn," but "forget not." David knew that the problem isn't ignorance. It's forgetfulness.

We are forgetful people. Israel forgot the Red Sea within days. Deuteronomy 8 warns them that when they enter the Promised Land, eat well and build good houses, they'll be tempted to say, "My power and the strength of my hands have produced this wealth for me." Moses answers, "But remember the Lord your God, for it is he who gives you the ability to produce wealth." Every salary, every harvest, every business that grew traces back to Him.

James says the same: "Every good and perfect gift is from above." Not some gifts. Every one. The job, the health, the school fees that were somehow found, the person who gave you a lift when you had no fare.

So let's do what David did and make a list. He names his benefits: forgiveness of sins, healing, redemption from the pit, love and compassion, and food that renews you like the eagle's youth. He's specific, and that's what makes it powerful.

And don't only thank God. Thank the people He used. Think of the parent who sacrificed, the teacher who stayed after class, the pastor who prayed for you, the friend who called when you were low. In many of our homes we save "asante" for large things. A short message today could mean more than you realise.

So tonight, take a page and write down 20 things God has done for you this year. Don't stop at 10. By number 15 you'll start to see things you'd forgotten. Then pick three people from your list and tell them thank you, today.

## Morning Prayer

Lord, I confess that I forget. I remember the problems easily and the miracles slowly. Open my memory today. Remind me of every benefit, the visible ones and the hidden ones, and of every person You used to carry me. Let my soul praise You and forget none of it.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read Psalm 103:1–5 slowly. List every benefit David names, then add your own beside each.
- Journal: write 20 things God has done for you this year. Don't stop at 10.
- Choose three people from your list and send each a short, specific thank-you message today.
