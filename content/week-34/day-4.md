## Revelation

Can you borrow someone else's faith?

Jesus told a story about ten young women waiting for a bridegroom to arrive for a wedding. From the outside, all ten looked exactly the same. They all had lamps. They were all dressed for the occasion. They were all waiting. And when the bridegroom was delayed, all ten of them fell asleep.

The difference didn't show up until midnight, when the shout rang out: "Here is the bridegroom! Come out to meet him." Five of them had brought extra oil, and five hadn't. The five whose lamps were going out turned to the others and said, "Give us some of your oil."

And the answer was no.

Now, that wasn't selfishness. It was reality. Some things can't be shared, and a relationship with God is one of them. Anyone who has sat through a blackout in Nairobi understands this. When the power goes off at night, you don't borrow your neighbour's charged torch. You need your own. Nobody can give you theirs without leaving themselves in the dark.

You can't ride into heaven on your grandmother's prayers. You can't borrow your spouse's faith. You can't get in on the strength of your church membership or the fact that you grew up in a Christian home. I've heard it said that God doesn't have any grandchildren. Every person has to come to Him on their own.

Here's what sobers me about this story. The foolish ones weren't pagans who had no interest in the wedding. They were part of the waiting crowd. They looked ready. They just never took care of the one thing that mattered, and when the door shut, it was too late to fix it. Jesus ends the story with a simple instruction: "Watch therefore, for you know neither the day nor the hour."

So let me ask you, gently but directly. Is there oil in your lamp? Not your family's lamp. Yours. If you're not sure, don't let another day go by. Settle it with Jesus right now. John says that to all who receive Him, "he gave the right to become children of God." That right is available to you today.

And if you do know Him, keep that lamp filled. Spend time with Him every day. Stay close. Because the bridegroom is coming, and when He does, you want to be ready to walk right in.

## Morning Prayer

Lord Jesus, I don't want to look ready and not be ready. I don't want to rely on my family's faith, my church's name or my own good record. Today I come to You for myself. Fill my lamp with Your Spirit, and keep me close to You every day until You come.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read Matthew 25:1–13. Notice that all ten looked the same on the outside, and only midnight showed the difference.
- Journal: "If I stood before Christ tonight, whose faith would I be relying on: my own, my family's, or my church's?" Write down where your trust really sits.
- If you're unsure about your own relationship with Jesus, settle it today. Tell Him, in your own words, that you trust Him. Then tell a pastor or a mature believer.
