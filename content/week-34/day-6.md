## Revelation

What are you supposed to be doing while you wait?

All week we've been talking about being ready for the Lord's return: stay awake, keep oil in the lamp, live as a child of the day. But some of us hear all that and picture a believer sitting on a hill with his eyes on the clouds, doing nothing until the trumpet sounds. That's not what Jesus describes.

He asks, "Who then is the faithful and wise servant, whom his master has set over his household, to give them their food at the proper time? Blessed is that servant whom his master will find so doing when he comes." Find him doing what? His job. Feeding the household. Ordinary, daily, unglamorous work.

Think of the askari at the gate of an estate. His job isn't dramatic. He opens the gate, checks who is coming in, walks around the compound in the dark hours and stays awake while everyone else sleeps. Nobody claps for him. But when the owner comes home unannounced at two in the morning, the gate opens at once and the compound is safe. That's what Jesus means: "Stay dressed for action and keep your lamps burning, and be like men who are waiting for their master to come home from the wedding feast, so that they may open the door to him at once when he comes and knocks."

Or think of the shop attendant whose employer is away for a week. Does she serve the customers honestly, keep the till right and lock up properly only when the boss is watching? Paul says, "it is required of stewards that they be found faithful." Not famous. Not successful. Faithful.

Here's the sobering part. Jesus warns about another servant, the one who says to himself, "My master is delayed," and begins to beat his fellow servants and to eat and drink with drunkards. Notice what happened. The delay became an excuse for neglect. That's the danger of hearing the alarm too many times. We start to think nothing is coming, and we stop doing what we were left to do.

So what has the Master left in your hands? A home to feed, a team to lead, a class to teach, a ministry to serve, a business to run honestly, a child to raise. Do it today as though He might walk in this evening. Don't wait for a dramatic assignment. The reward goes to the one He finds "so doing". And Jesus promises, "Truly, I say to you, he will set him over all his possessions."

## Morning Prayer

Lord, I don't want to just wait for You. I want to be found doing what You gave me to do. Make me faithful in the small, unseen tasks, whether anyone is watching or not. Keep me from using Your delay as an excuse for neglect, and let me be a servant You are glad to find at work when You come.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read Matthew 24:42–51. Compare the two servants. What did the delay do to each of them?
- Journal: "What has the Master put in my hands right now: at home, at work and in the church?" Choose one task you have been neglecting and do it first thing today.
- Do one piece of work today with the same care you'd give it if your employer, or the Lord Himself, were standing behind you.
