## Revelation

How much of the Bible is living in your house?

I don't mean how many Bibles you own. Most of us have several, along with a few apps. I mean how much of God's Word is actually living in you, shaping what you say, how you work, and how you treat the people around you.

Paul says, "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly." The word for "dwell" means to make a home. A visitor drops in on Sunday morning and leaves by lunch, but someone who dwells has his shoes by the door and his name on the mail. Too many of us treat God's Word as a Sunday guest.

Look back at this week. We started with work, giving our hands to God with all our might. We moved to our words and a coal from the altar. We learned to comfort the grieving, to ask God to bless what we do, to forgive as we've been forgiven, and to put on love. Every one of those things comes from the Word dwelling richly. Out of a full heart, the mouth speaks and the hands work. You can't give out what hasn't gone in.

Notice also what Paul says happens when the Word dwells in you. You teach and encourage one another. You sing. You give thanks. The Word doesn't stay inside. It comes out in conversations, in songs, and in gratitude.

And notice the word "richly." Not a verse a day grabbed in a hurry, but a deep, steady, daily intake. Memorize a passage. Read a chapter out loud. Sing the hymn in the car, even if you can't carry a tune. God isn't grading your voice.

So today, as you gather with God's people, listen like someone who plans to take it home. When the service ends, ask yourself what you'll carry out the door. Pick one verse from this week, write it on a card, and put it where you'll see it tomorrow morning. Then let it move in.

## Morning Prayer

Lord, I don't want Your Word to be a Sunday guest. Let it move in and make a home in me. Fill my heart so that what comes out of my mouth, my hands, and my songs is Yours. As I gather with Your people today, open my ears to hear, and help me carry what I've heard into Monday morning with thankfulness.

## Daily Practices

- Read Colossians 3:12–17 as one passage, out loud. Notice how every instruction in it flows from the Word dwelling richly.
- Journal: "Which verse from this week do I want to carry into the next one?" Write it on a card and put it where you will see it tomorrow.
- Sing or hum one hymn or worship song today, even if only in the car, and thank God for something specific.
