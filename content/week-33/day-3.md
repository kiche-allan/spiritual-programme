## Revelation

When was the last time you were overwhelmed that Jesus loves you?

Most of us learned it as little children: "Jesus loves me, this I know, for the Bible tells me so." We sang it in Sunday school without thinking twice. But as you grow older and wiser in the Word, you begin to realise what that little song actually means. God loves me!

To understand how big that is, you have to understand what He saved you from. Our God is a God of love. Amen? But He is also a God of wrath. It has been said that modern people take Christianity lightly because they take the wrath of God too lightly. God's wrath is what happens when people refuse the love He has offered in Christ Jesus.

But God provided a way out. Jesus went to the cross and shed His blood to cover the sin you couldn't cover. He died the death you deserved. He took the wrath of God so you wouldn't have to. Paul says that if you've trusted Christ, you have been justified by His blood, and you will be saved from the wrath of God. "Justified" is a courtroom word. The Judge declares you not guilty, and the verdict stands because Someone else paid the price.

And He didn't do it because you went looking for Him. John says, "This is love: not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son." Your salvation started with God. He drew you, He changed you, and He made you His. Paul says that even when we were dead in our transgressions, God, rich in mercy, made us alive with Christ. Dead people don't reach out. He reached first.

I don't know about you, but I'm overwhelmed that Jesus would love me. Why me? And I know some of you are looking at me thinking, "Yeah, why you?" Fair enough! But the real question is, why you? The only answer is that He loves you. Not because you were clever, good or religious. Because He loves you.

Don't let that truth get old. We can sing a song for twenty years and stop hearing it. Sing that little song out loud today, slowly, like you mean it. Then thank Him by name for the specific sins He has carried for you, and live this day grateful that, in Christ, you will never face His wrath.

And if you've never trusted Him, don't put it off. The same blood that covers me is offered to you today.

## Morning Prayer

Lord Jesus, I have sung that I am loved for years, and today I want to hear it again. Thank You that You took the wrath I deserved. Thank You that You loved me first, when I wasn't looking for You. Forgive me for taking Your love lightly. I thank You by name for the sins You have carried, and I choose to live this day grateful and unafraid.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read Romans 5:6–11 slowly. Underline every word that describes what we were and every word that describes what Christ did.
- Journal: "What specific sins has Jesus carried for me that I have stopped thanking Him for?" Write them down, then write across the page: "Paid in full."
- Sing or say "Jesus loves me, this I know" aloud today, slowly. Then tell one person that Jesus loves them.
