## Revelation

Are you trying to be someone you are not?

There is an exhausting performance that many believers maintain — the version of themselves that shows up to church, posts on social media, and manages first impressions. Polished. Put-together. Spiritually sorted. And then there is the actual person — the one who is struggling, uncertain, and nowhere near as consistent as the performance suggests.

God is not impressed by the performance. He already knows the actual person. And the gap between who you are presenting and who you actually are is not a gap that can be sustained indefinitely — it costs too much and produces too little.

God does not need another copy of someone else. He needs you — the real you. Unpolished if necessary, but genuine. The person He designed and called and placed specifically in your relationships and your community is the real version, not the managed one.

Stop performing. The people around you need your authenticity more than they need your highlight reel. Your honesty about your struggle gives other struggling people permission to be real too. Your willingness to be known — actually known — is one of the most powerful things you can bring to any room you enter.

That is where God can actually use you. Not the version you constructed. The one He made.

## Morning Prayer

Father, I am tired of the performance. I am tired of managing the gap between who I am presenting and who I actually am. Today I choose to be real — with You, and with the people You have placed around me. You already know everything I have been hiding from the room. And You love me anyway. Let that love be the ground I stand on as I stop performing and start being present — actually present, as I actually am.

## Daily Practices

- Read Psalm 139:1–4 — You have searched me, Lord, and You know me. You know when I sit and when I rise. You perceive my thoughts from afar. God's knowledge of you is complete and prior — He is not waiting for you to reveal yourself to Him. Ask: what am I still performing for God that He has already seen through?
- Journal: "What is the gap between the version of myself I present and the version I actually am? What would it cost me to close that gap — to be the same person in every room?" Write the honest answer.
- Read John 4:16–18 — Jesus and the woman at the well. He named what she had been hiding before she told Him. And the conversation that followed changed her life. Ask: what is the thing Jesus already knows about me that I have not yet brought into the open with Him?
