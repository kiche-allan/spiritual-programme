## Revelation

You carry a weapon with you everywhere you go. You use it dozens of times every day — in conversations, in messages, in the things you say to yourself when no one is listening. And Proverbs 18:21 says it carries the power of life and death.

The tongue is not neutral. Every time you open your mouth something is happening — something is being built or torn down, life is being released or death is being spoken. The person who understands this lives differently. They choose words deliberately — not because they are performing politeness but because they know words carry weight.

James 3 calls the tongue a fire that can set a whole forest ablaze from a single spark. A small thing with enormous consequences. And the warning is not only about what you say to others. It is about consistency — the same mouth that praises God on Sunday cannot curse the people made in His image on Monday (James 3:9–10).

What are you speaking over your own life? Over your children? Over the people in your circle? The words you repeat most become the atmosphere the people around you live in. That atmosphere is yours to set.

Make it life.

## Morning Prayer

Father, set a guard over my mouth today. Let what comes out of it build rather than break — in the conversations I plan and the ones I do not. Where I have been speaking death through complaint, criticism, and careless words, I repent. Give me the discipline today to ask before I speak: is this life or death? And let me choose life — every time.

## Amen

In Jesus' name, Amen.

## Daily Practices

- Read James 3:1–12 in full. Ask: what is the pattern of words I repeat most — and are they producing life or death in the people who hear them?
- Journal: "What am I consistently speaking over my own life that contradicts what God says about me? Write three specific exchanges — the word you have been speaking, and the word God speaks instead."
- Practise one hour of intentional speech today. Before you say anything difficult, ask: is this building or tearing down?
