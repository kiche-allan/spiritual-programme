## Revelation

Is there anything in your life right now that you would be embarrassed to ask God to bless?

Here's a simple question I've learned to ask myself over the years, and it has saved me from a lot of foolishness. If I cannot ask God to bless what I am doing or what I am saying, maybe I ought not to be doing it or saying it.

That one question clears things up in a hurry. Try it on the deal you're about to sign at work. Try it on the show you're about to turn on tonight. Try it on the text message you're about to send while you're angry. Picture yourself bowing your head and saying, "Lord, would You bless this?" If the words stick in your throat, you already have your answer.

Paul tells us to do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus. That means you're signing His name to it. When you represent a company, you don't sign contracts the company would never approve. When you belong to Christ, you're representing Him in the boardroom, the tea break, and the family room.

Many believers live with one set of rules on Sunday and another on Monday. We come to church and sing about holiness, then go do business like everybody else. That's called hypocrisy, by the way. You can't live a splintered life without getting full of splinters, and splinters have a way of sticking other people the wrong way.

Notice also that Paul says "in word or deed." It covers the whole of life, from the big decision to the throwaway comment. Nothing falls outside it, and that's good news, because it means nothing in your life is too small for God to care about.

The test also sets you free. When you can honestly ask God to bless what you're doing, you don't have to look over your shoulder. You can work with a clear conscience and a thankful heart.

So before your next decision today, stop and ask the question. Can I ask God to bless this? If you can, go for it with everything you've got. If you can't, let it go and ask Him what He'd rather have you do.

## Morning Prayer

Father, search me and show me anything in my life I would be embarrassed to ask You to bless. I don't want a Sunday faith and a Monday life. Whatever I say and whatever I do today, let me do it in the name of the Lord Jesus, with a clear conscience and a thankful heart. Where I cannot ask for Your blessing, give me the courage to let it go.

## Daily Practices

- Read Colossians 3:12–17. Notice how many times Paul links everyday life with the name of Jesus and with thanksgiving.
- Journal: "What decision, habit, or conversation am I avoiding bringing to God for His blessing?" Be honest, then decide what to do with it.
- Before each major decision today, pray one sentence: "Lord, would You bless this?" Act only on the answer you can honestly give.
